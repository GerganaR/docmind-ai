# Acme Analytics Employee Handbook (fictional sample document)

This handbook applies to all permanent employees of Acme Analytics GmbH. Acme Analytics is a fictional company created for demonstration purposes.

## Working Hours

The standard working week is 38.5 hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 CET, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees may organise their time flexibly, provided that team commitments and customer meetings are respected. Overtime must be approved in advance by the employee's line manager and is compensated with time off in lieu.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams agree on at least one shared office day per week, usually Tuesday or Wednesday. Working from another country is allowed for a maximum of 20 working days per calendar year and requires written approval from HR at least two weeks in advance, because of tax and social-security rules.

Remote workers receive a one-time home office allowance of 500 EUR for equipment such as a chair, monitor or desk. Receipts must be submitted through the expense system.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid vacation per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive vacation days pro rata. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March. Vacation requests of more than five consecutive days should be submitted at least four weeks in advance.

Sick leave must be reported to the line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day of illness.

## Parental Leave

Acme Analytics follows statutory parental leave rules. In addition, the company offers four weeks of fully paid leave to the second parent, which can be taken within the first six months after birth or adoption.

## Expenses and Travel

Business expenses are reimbursed when they are submitted with a receipt within 30 days. Any single expense above 250 EUR requires pre-approval from the line manager. Train travel is preferred for trips under four hours; economy class is the default for flights. The daily meal allowance during business travel is 40 EUR.

## Learning and Development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 EUR and three learning days per year. The budget can be used for courses, certifications, conferences and books. Unused learning budget does not carry over.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in January and July. Salary reviews happen once a year in January and are based on the review outcome, market data and company performance.
