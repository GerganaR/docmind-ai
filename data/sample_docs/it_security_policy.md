# Acme Analytics IT Security Policy (fictional sample document)

Version 2.1. Owner: Information Security Team. This policy applies to all employees, contractors and interns.

## Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Employees must use the company password manager to generate and store passwords. Passwords must never be shared, written down or reused across services. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, the code repository and all customer-facing systems. Hardware security keys are required for administrators.

## Devices

All company laptops use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system and browser updates must be installed within 7 days of release. Personal devices may only access company email through the managed mobile app; they must not store customer data.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk immediately so the device can be wiped remotely.

## Data Classification

Data is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Customer data is always classified as Confidential or higher. Restricted data, such as payment details and health information, may only be stored in approved systems located in the EU. Confidential data must not be pasted into public AI tools or unapproved third-party services.

## Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident, including phishing emails, malware, or accidental data disclosure, must be reported to security@acme-analytics.example within one hour of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate the incident yourself and do not delete suspicious emails; forward them as attachments to the security team. The security team acknowledges every report within 30 minutes during business hours.

## Access Control

Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed every quarter by each team lead. When an employee leaves the company, all accounts are disabled on their last working day. Shared accounts are not permitted.

## Security Training

All staff complete security awareness training during onboarding and a refresher course every year. Simulated phishing exercises are run quarterly.
